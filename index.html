About Our School

O.B. Montessori Center is a progressive co-educational school in the Philippines that follows the educational philosophy of Dr. Maria Montessori.

The school aims to develop independent, responsible, and productive individuals. Students are encouraged to explore their interests, develop their abilities, and become lifelong learners.

Enrollment Requirements

Prepare the following documents before proceeding with your enrollment.

Student Requirements
  • Completed application form
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Previous school records
  • Recent student photograph
  • Report card or academic records
Parent / Guardian Requirements
  • Parent or guardian information
  • Valid contact number
  • Active email address
  • Additional documents requested by the school
